# Étude des propriétés magnétiques: α-Fe₂O₃ et γ-Fe₂O₃

## Abstract

This study examines the magnetic properties of **α-Fe₂O₃** (hematite) and **γ-Fe₂O₃** (maghemite) nanoparticles synthesized via co-precipitation.

## Results

Mössbauer spectroscopy confirmed the phase purity. The Néel temperature T_N = 948 K for α-Fe₂O₃ agreed with literature values within ±2 K.
