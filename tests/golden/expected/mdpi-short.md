# Electrochemical Sensors for Heavy Metal Detection in Water

## Abstract

We developed a **graphene-modified electrode** for simultaneous detection of Pb^2+, Cd^2+, and Hg^2+ in aqueous samples.

## Results

Detection limits achieved were 0.1 ppb for Pb^2+, 0.05 ppb for Cd^2+, and 0.2 ppb for Hg^2+, well below WHO guidelines.

The sensor demonstrated *excellent selectivity* in the presence of common interfering ions.
