# Methodological Issues in Sociolinguistic Research

## Data Collection in Sociolinguistics

Sociolinguistic research requires careful attention to the **observer's paradox**.

## Interview Techniques

Labov (1972) identified several strategies for eliciting natural speech:

- Danger of death narratives
- Childhood games and rhymes
- Peer group interaction

### Ethical Considerations

Informed consent must balance transparency with the need for *naturalistic* data collection.
