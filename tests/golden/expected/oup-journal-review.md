# Epigenetic Regulation of Gene Expression

## Abstract

DNA methylation and histone modification are key mechanisms of **epigenetic regulation**.

## Mechanisms of Epigenetic Control

### DNA Methylation

CpG islands in promoter regions, when methylated by *DNMT3A/B*, silence gene expression.

### Histone Modifications

The histone code includes:

- H3K4me3 — active transcription
- H3K27me3 — Polycomb-mediated silencing
- H3K9me3 — heterochromatin formation
