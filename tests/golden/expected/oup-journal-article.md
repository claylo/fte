# Genomic Surveillance of Antimicrobial Resistance

## Abstract

Whole-genome sequencing enables rapid detection of antimicrobial resistance (AMR) determinants.

## Introduction

Antimicrobial resistance is a **global health threat**. The WHO estimates that AMR contributed to 4.95 million deaths in 2019^1.

## Methods

We analyzed 1,247 *Staphylococcus aureus* isolates collected across 15 hospitals.

| --- | --- | --- |
| mecA | Methicillin | 34.2 |
| vanA | Vancomycin | 2.1 |
| ermB | Erythromycin | 18.7 |

## Discussion

Our results confirm the utility of WGS for **real-time** AMR surveillance in clinical settings.
