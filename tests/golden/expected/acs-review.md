# Metal-Organic Frameworks for Gas Separation: A Review

## Introduction

Metal-organic frameworks (MOFs) are **crystalline porous materials** assembled from metal nodes and organic linkers.

## Design Principles

### Pore Size Engineering

The pore aperture can be tuned by varying the linker length, enabling *molecular sieving* of gas mixtures.

### Open Metal Sites

Coordinatively unsaturated metal centers enhance selectivity for polar molecules:

- CO_2 over N_2
- H_2O over organic vapors
- Olefins over paraffins
