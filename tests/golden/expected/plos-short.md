# A Brief Communication on Coral Bleaching

## Abstract

Mass coral bleaching events have increased in **frequency** and **severity** since the 1980s.

## Findings

Sea surface temperatures exceeding 1°C above the *maximum monthly mean* triggered bleaching in 94% of surveyed reefs.

Recovery time averaged 8–12 years, but only when thermal stress did **not** recur within the recovery window.
