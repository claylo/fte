# "Click" Chemistry: CuAAC Reactions in Water

## Abstract

We demonstrate aqueous copper(I)-catalyzed azide–alkyne cycloaddition (CuAAC) using a water-soluble **tris(triazolylmethyl)amine** ligand with turnover numbers exceeding 10^4.

## Results

The reaction tolerates a wide range of functional groups including −OH, −NH_2, −CO_2H, and −SH without protection.

| Azide | Alkyne | Time (h) | Yield (%) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BnN3 | PhC≡CH | 1 | 99 |
| Glycosyl-N3 | Propargyl-OH | 2 | 95 |
| PEG-N3 | Biotin-≡ | 4 | 88 |
