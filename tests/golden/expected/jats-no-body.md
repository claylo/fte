# Evidence for a Ninth Planet in the Outer Solar System

## Abstract

Orbital clustering of extreme trans-Neptunian objects suggests gravitational perturbation by an unseen planet with mass 5–10 M_Earth at a semimajor axis of 400–800 AU.
