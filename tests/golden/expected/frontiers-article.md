# Neural Correlates of Decision-Making Under Uncertainty

## Abstract

Using functional MRI, we investigated **neural correlates** of decision-making under varying levels of uncertainty in 48 healthy adults.

## Introduction

Decision-making under uncertainty engages a network of brain regions including the *prefrontal cortex*, anterior insula, and striatum.

## Materials and Methods

### Participants

Forty-eight right-handed adults (24 female, mean age 28.4 years) were recruited.

### Task Design

Participants completed a probabilistic gambling task with three uncertainty levels:

- **Low uncertainty** — 80/20 reward probabilities
- **Medium uncertainty** — 60/40 reward probabilities
- **High uncertainty** — 50/50 reward probabilities

## Results

| Region | Low | Medium | High |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| dlPFC | 2.31 | 3.87 | 5.12 |
| Insula | 1.89 | 3.42 | 4.76 |
| Striatum | 3.15 | 2.98 | 2.21 |

## Discussion

Activity in the **dorsolateral prefrontal cortex** scaled linearly with uncertainty, consistent with its role in cognitive control.
