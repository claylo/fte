# Epidemiology of Hospital-Acquired Infections in Sub-Saharan Africa

## Abstract

**Background** Hospital-acquired infections (HAIs) are a major burden in resource-limited settings.

**Methods** We conducted a prospective surveillance study across 12 hospitals in Ghana and Senegal.

**Results** The overall HAI rate was 14.2 per 100 admissions, with surgical site infections accounting for 38%.

## Introduction

Healthcare-associated infections remain a *critical* challenge in low- and middle-income countries, where infection prevention resources are limited.

## Results

The most common pathogens were *Klebsiella pneumoniae* (28%), *Staphylococcus aureus* (22%), and *Escherichia coli* (18%).
