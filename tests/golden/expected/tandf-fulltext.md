# Cognitive Load Theory in Online Learning Environments

## Abstract

This study investigates **cognitive load** in multimedia learning environments, drawing on *Sweller's cognitive load theory*.

## Theoretical Framework

Cognitive load theory distinguishes three types of load:

- **Intrinsic load** — inherent complexity of the material
- **Extraneous load** — caused by poor instructional design
- **Germane load** — effort devoted to schema construction

## Method

### Participants

A total of 240 undergraduate students (M_age = 21.3, SD = 2.1) participated in the study.

### Measures

| Measure | Scale | α |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Intrinsic load | 1–9 | .87 |
| Extraneous load | 1–9 | .82 |
| Germane load | 1–9 | .79 |

## Discussion

Results support the *redundancy effect*: presenting identical information in multiple modalities increased extraneous load without improving learning outcomes.
