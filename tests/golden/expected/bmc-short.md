# Brief Report: Vitamin D Status in Pregnant Women

## Abstract

Vitamin D deficiency during pregnancy is associated with adverse outcomes including **preeclampsia** and *gestational diabetes*.

## Findings

Among 320 participants, 42% had serum 25(OH)D levels below 30 nmol/L.

Supplementation with 4000 IU/day restored adequate levels in 89% of deficient women by the third trimester.
