# Plant Responses to Drought Stress: Molecular Mechanisms

## Introduction

Drought is the most significant **abiotic stress** affecting crop productivity worldwide.

## Signaling Pathways

### ABA-Dependent Pathway

Abscisic acid (ABA) accumulation triggers stomatal closure through *guard cell* ion channel regulation.

### ABA-Independent Pathway

DREB/CBF transcription factors activate drought-responsive genes without requiring ABA signaling.

## Adaptive Responses

Plants deploy multiple strategies:

2. Root architecture remodeling for deeper water access
4. Osmolyte accumulation (proline, glycine betaine)
6. Cuticular wax deposition to reduce transpiration
