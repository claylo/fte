# Gut Microbiome Diversity in Inflammatory Bowel Disease

## Abstract

We characterized **gut microbiome** composition in 500 patients with inflammatory bowel disease (IBD) using 16S rRNA sequencing.

## Background

The human gut harbors a complex microbial ecosystem. *Dysbiosis* — an imbalance in microbial communities — is implicated in IBD pathogenesis.

## Methods

### Patient Cohort

Patients were recruited from three tertiary care centers between 2020 and 2023.

### Sequencing

DNA was extracted and the V3–V4 region of the 16S rRNA gene was amplified.

## Results

Alpha diversity was significantly reduced in IBD patients compared to controls:

| Group | Shannon Index | Observed OTUs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Control | 3.82 | 412 |
| Crohn's | 2.41 | 267 |
| UC | 2.89 | 301 |

## Conclusions

Our findings confirm reduced microbial diversity in IBD, with **Crohn's disease** showing greater dysbiosis than ulcerative colitis.
