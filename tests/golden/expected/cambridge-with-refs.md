# Sediment Transport in Braided Rivers

## Abstract

Field measurements of **bedload transport** in a braided gravel-bed river reveal intermittent transport pulses linked to bar migration.

## Introduction

Braided rivers are characterized by multiple channels separated by exposed bars. The *morphodynamics* of bar migration control sediment flux at reach scales.

## Field Site

The Waimakariri River, Canterbury, New Zealand, was chosen for its accessibility and well-documented hydrology.

## Discussion

The pulsed nature of transport is consistent with **stochastic models** of bed-surface grain entrainment.

> The relationship between flow and transport in gravel-bed rivers is fundamentally non-linear, mediated by bed armoring and supply limitation.
