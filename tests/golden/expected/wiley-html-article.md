# Molecular Dynamics of Protein Folding

## Abstract

We present a **novel computational framework** for studying protein folding dynamics using molecular dynamics simulations.

## Introduction

Protein folding remains one of the grand challenges in structural biology. The *Levinthal paradox* highlights that proteins fold on biologically relevant timescales despite astronomical conformational spaces.

## Computational Methods

Simulations were performed using the following parameters:

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Force field | AMBER ff14SB |
| Time step | 2 fs |
| Temperature | 300 K |

## Results

### Folding Pathways

Three distinct folding pathways were identified for the **WW domain**:

- Direct folding via hairpin nucleation
- Indirect pathway through a *molten globule* intermediate
- Misfolded traps requiring partial unfolding
