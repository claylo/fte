# Synthesis of β-Lactam Antibiotics via Enzymatic Cascade

## Abstract

A three-enzyme cascade converts simple *α*-amino acids to ***β*-lactam** scaffolds in a single pot with >90% diastereoselectivity.

## Introduction

The β-lactam ring is the pharmacophore of penicillins, cephalosporins, and carbapenems. Conventional synthesis requires multiple steps and harsh conditions. **Biocatalytic routes** offer mild, selective alternatives.

## Results

### Enzyme Screening

We tested 15 isopenicillin N synthase (IPNS) variants. The **triple mutant** *IPNS*^F235L/T106A/R279Q showed the highest activity.

### Substrate Scope

Both L- and D-configured substrates were accepted:

- L-Cysteine derivatives: 92–98% yield
- D-Valine analogues: 78–85% yield
- *N*-Methylated substrates: 45–62% yield (reduced due to steric clash)

| Substrate | Product | Yield (%) | dr | ee (%) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ACV tripeptide | Isopenicillin N | 95 | >20:1 | >99 |
| Modified ACV | 6-APA analogue | 87 | 15:1 | 98 |
| N-Me-ACV | N-Me-penam | 52 | 8:1 | 95 |

## Conclusion

This cascade provides **gram-scale access** to β-lactams without protecting groups, heavy metals, or cryogenic conditions.
