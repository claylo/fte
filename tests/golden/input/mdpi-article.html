Introduction

Global plastic waste production exceeds 350 million tonnes annually. Biodegradable polymers offer a pathway toward sustainable materials management.

Classification of Biodegradable Polymers

Bio-based Polymers

Polymers derived from renewable resources include:

  • Polylactic acid (PLA) — from corn starch fermentation
  • Polyhydroxyalkanoates (PHA) — from bacterial biosynthesis
  • Starch-based blends — from native or modified starch

Petroleum-based Biodegradables

PBAT and PCL degrade under composting conditions despite their fossil origin.

Conclusions

PLA remains the most commercially viable biodegradable polymer, though PHA shows superior marine degradability.
