Perception and Consciousness

The relationship between perception and consciousness has been debated since Descartes.

Philosophical Frameworks

Three major positions dominate the literature:

  1. Direct realism — we perceive objects as they are
  2. Indirect realism — perception is mediated by mental representations
  3. Idealism — objects are mind-dependent

The Hard Problem

Chalmers (1995) distinguished the hard problem of consciousness — why there is subjective experience — from the easy problems of explaining cognitive functions.
