Abstract

We report a copper-catalyzed method for direct C–H functionalization of indoles and pyrroles with broad substrate scope.

Introduction

Transition-metal-catalyzed C–H activation has emerged as a powerful strategy for constructing C–C and C–heteroatom bonds. The regioselectivity of C–H functionalization remains a key challenge.

Results and Discussion

Optimization of reaction conditions revealed that Cu(OAc)2 with 1,10-phenanthroline as ligand provided optimal yields.
