Abstract

We developed a graphene-modified electrode for simultaneous detection of Pb2+, Cd2+, and Hg2+ in aqueous samples.

Results

Detection limits achieved were 0.1 ppb for Pb2+, 0.05 ppb for Cd2+, and 0.2 ppb for Hg2+, well below WHO guidelines.

The sensor demonstrated excellent selectivity in the presence of common interfering ions.
