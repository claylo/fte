Introduction

Perovskite solar cells have achieved power conversion efficiencies exceeding 26%, rivaling crystalline silicon.

Material Properties

The ABX3 perovskite structure offers tunable bandgaps from 1.2 to 2.3 eV by varying the halide composition.

Stability Challenges

Degradation under moisture, heat, and light remains the primary barrier to commercialization. Key mechanisms include:

  • Ion migration under electric fields
  • Phase segregation in mixed-halide compositions
  • Decomposition of the organic cation

Device Architectures

Both n-i-p and p-i-n configurations are actively explored, with inverted (p-i-n) devices showing superior stability.
