Abstract

We demonstrate aqueous copper(I)-catalyzed azide–alkyne cycloaddition (CuAAC) using a water-soluble tris(triazolylmethyl)amine ligand with turnover numbers exceeding 104.

Results

The reaction tolerates a wide range of functional groups including −OH, −NH2, −CO2H, and −SH without protection.
