Overview

This chapter introduces the Fourier transform and its applications in digital signal processing.

Discrete Fourier Transform

The DFT converts a finite sequence of samples into frequency components. For a sequence x[n] of length N:

  1. Compute the real component
  2. Compute the imaginary component
  3. Combine using Euler's formula: eiθ = cos θ + i sin θ

Applications

Signal processing finds use in audio compression, image analysis, and telecommunications.
