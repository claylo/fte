Abstract

This study examines the effects of temperature on enzyme kinetics in Escherichia coli.

Introduction

Enzyme kinetics are fundamental to understanding cellular metabolism. Previous work by Johnson1 demonstrated that Vmax increases with temperature up to a critical threshold.

Methods

Sample Preparation

Cultures were grown in LB broth at 37°C for 24 hours.

Assay Protocol

We measured reaction rates under three conditions:

  • Low temperature (20°C)
  • Optimal temperature (37°C)
  • High temperature (50°C)

Results

Discussion

Our findings are consistent with Arrhenius theory, which predicts an exponential increase in reaction rate with temperature.

The relationship between temperature and enzyme activity is one of the most fundamental in biochemistry.
