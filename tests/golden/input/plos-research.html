Abstract

We used social network analysis to characterize association patterns in bottlenose dolphins (Tursiops truncatus).

Introduction

Cetacean social structure varies widely across species. Bottlenose dolphins form fission-fusion societies in which group composition changes over hours to days.

Methods

Study Area

Photo-identification surveys were conducted in Shark Bay, Western Australia, from 2018 to 2023.

Network Metrics

We calculated the following centrality measures:

  • Degree centrality
  • Betweenness centrality
  • Eigenvector centrality

Discussion

Community structure was stable across years, suggesting long-term social bonds drive group formation.
