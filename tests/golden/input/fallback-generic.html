Introduction

The transition to open access publishing has accelerated since 2020, driven by funder mandates and institutional policies.

Models of Open Access

Four primary models exist:

  1. Gold OA — published openly, often with an APC
  2. Green OA — self-archived in a repository
  3. Diamond OA — no fees to authors or readers
  4. Hybrid — subscription journal with optional OA per article

Impact on Citation Rates

Meta-analyses consistently show an open access citation advantage, though the magnitude varies by discipline.
