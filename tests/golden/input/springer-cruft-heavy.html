Abstract

We model nanoparticle transport through fractured sandstone using pore-network simulations.

Introduction

Understanding colloid transport in subsurface environments is critical for groundwater remediation and enhanced oil recovery.

Numerical Method

Pore Network Construction

Networks were extracted from micro-CT images at 2 μm resolution.

Results

Breakthrough Curves

Early breakthrough occurred in all fractured samples due to preferential flow paths.

Effect of Fracture Aperture

Wider fractures (>100 μm) showed anomalous transport with heavy-tailed breakthrough curves.

Sub-analysis of Tail Behavior

Power-law tails with exponent α ≈ 1.5 suggest matrix diffusion as the dominant trapping mechanism.
