Abstract

Whole-genome sequencing enables rapid detection of antimicrobial resistance (AMR) determinants.

Introduction

Antimicrobial resistance is a global health threat. The WHO estimates that AMR contributed to 4.95 million deaths in 20191.

Methods

We analyzed 1,247 Staphylococcus aureus isolates collected across 15 hospitals.
